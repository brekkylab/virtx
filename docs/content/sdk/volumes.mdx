A volume keeps what a sandbox writes for the next one. A `Volume` has its `name` and `size` (in Rust, `name()` and `info()`), and its [files](/docs/sdk/files/).
See [Mounting volumes](/docs/tutorials/mounting-volumes/).

| Member | Does |
| --- | --- |
| [`create_volume`](#create_volume) | Makes an empty volume. |
| [`list_volume`](#list_volume) | The server's volumes, or the one named `name`. |
| [`mount`](#mount) | Mounts a remote store at `path` in it, read-only: a Notion integration's pages, a Google Drive or a OneDrive. |
| [`mounts`](#mounts) | The mounts in it, without their credentials. |
| [`unmount`](#unmount) | Takes a mount away, leaving its store as it is. |
| [`remove`](#remove) | Deletes it and everything in it. |

## Errors

A call that fails raises a [`VirtxError`](/docs/sdk/errors/) in Python, throws one in Node.js, and returns one in Rust. Branch on its `status` and `code`, not its message. These are the errors the calls on this page return most often.

| Error | From | Cause |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `409`, `exists` | `create_volume` | Another volume has that name. |
| `404`, `not_found` | Any call naming a volume | No volume has that name. Check it against `list_volume`. |
| `409`, `exists` | `mount` | A store is already mounted at that path. `unmount` it first, or use another path. |
| `400`, `invalid`, `… would be inside or over the mount at …` | `mount` | A mount inside or over another one, or at the volume's root. Mount each store in its own subdirectory. |
| `400`, `invalid`, `making the mount's store: …` | `mount` | The store couldn't be made from what was given, such as malformed credentials. `mount` doesn't contact the store, so a wrong key isn't caught here: it fails later, when something in the mount is read. |
| `403` | `remove_file`, `rename_file`, `copy_file` | The path is or holds a mount, or a copy lands on one. `unmount` it first. |
| `400`, `invalid`, `… another volume is there` | `create_sandbox` with `volumes` | Two volumes are mounted at one path. Give each its own path. |
| `409`, `in_use` | `remove` | A sandbox still mounts the volume. Remove that sandbox first. |

Any call can also fail with `401`, `unauthorized` or `403`, `forbidden` (see [Client](/docs/sdk/client/#errors)), or with `source` `transport` when the connection drops. The client retries `503`, `capacity` and a `429`, `quota` for the request rate by itself for up to 30 seconds: see [Retries](/docs/sdk/errors/#retries).
